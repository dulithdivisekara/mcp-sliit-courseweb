Frequently Asked Questions

      


      

        

          
            Is my SLIIT password or IT number sent anywhere?
            +
          
          

            No. The MCP server runs 100% locally on your computer over standard I/O (stdio). Your credentials and cookies are stored only in your local .env and storage_state.json.
          

        


        

          
            What if I have Microsoft Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) enabled?
            +
          
          

            Run the interactive_login tool. It launches a visible browser window, pre-fills your email, and pauses so you can approve the prompt on your Microsoft Authenticator app.
          

        


        

          
            How can I download all lecture slides from Courseweb automatically?
            +
          
          

            Using the sync_module tool, your AI assistant scans the Moodle section hierarchy for any enrolled module, detects all lecture slides, tutorials, and lab PDFs, and downloads them neatly organized into local folders in one command.
          

        


        

          
            Can AI alert me about upcoming Courseweb assignment deadlines?
            +
          
          

            Yes. The get_deadlines tool extracts all upcoming submission cutoffs, quizzes, and project deliverables across all your subjects on Courseweb and presents them in a prioritized, chronological list.
          

        


        

          
            How does it find my marks inside a 100+ page exam PDF?
            +
          
          

            Instead of manually searching row-by-row on your phone, your AI assistant downloads the published results PDF, parses the tabular data, matches your specific IT number, and extracts your exact score and grade breakdown in seconds.
          

        


        

          
            Does this violate SLIIT IT policies?
            +
          
          

            This tool is built strictly for personal educational convenience and respects campus servers with reasonable request intervals. Personal educational use only.
